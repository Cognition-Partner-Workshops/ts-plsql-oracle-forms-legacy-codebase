Heritage Weaving

Kanchipuram sarees have been handwoven for over 400 years in the temple town of Kanchipuram, Tamil Nadu. Each saree takes 15-20 days to complete using traditional pit looms.

Saree Care Guide

Storage

  • Store in a cool, dry place wrapped in muslin cloth
  • Refold every 3-6 months to prevent crease marks
  • Keep silica gel packets to absorb moisture
  • Store separately from cotton garments

Cleaning

  • Dry clean only for pure silk sarees
  • Avoid exposure to direct sunlight for extended periods
  • Spot clean minor stains with mild soap and cold water
  • Never wring or twist the fabric

Ironing

  • Iron on low heat with a pressing cloth
  • Steam iron is preferred for silk sarees
  • Never iron directly on zari work
  • Iron on the reverse side to preserve sheen

Buying Tips

  • Look for Silk Mark certification for authenticity
  • Check if it is handloom or powerloom
  • Verify the weight (pure silk is heavier)
  • Burn test: pure silk smells like burnt hair
